      What it does

      Find a recipe, cook it, plan the week, keep what you love.

      Find

      • Search every recipe by name, ingredient, cookbook or your own tags.
      • Filter by course, protein, diet, method, time and rating, across your cookbooks and the recipe sites.
      • Air fryer, slow cooker, Instant Pot, sheet pan and freezer-friendly are detected for you.
      • Leave out what you don't like: “without mushrooms, cilantro”.
      • Your most-used filters, like “★ 5 · 30 min or less”, are one tap away.

      Cook

      • Cook mode keeps the screen awake and makes the text bigger.
      • Check off ingredients and highlight the step you're on.
      • Tap any time in a step to start a timer with an alarm.
      • Scale to half, double or triple.

      Plan

      • Pick a day on a calendar and add a recipe, or just write the meal: “Leftovers”, “Pizza night”, “Tacos at Grandma's”.
      • See the week at a glance, with breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks.
      • Add meals to Google Calendar, or export the week in one file.
      • Build a shopping list from a recipe or the whole week, sorted by store aisle, then copy or share it.

      Keep

      • Star your favorites and find them, with everything you've saved from the web, in one Saved tab.
      • Rate recipes yourself and filter by your own stars.
      • Tag recipes your way: Kid-approved, Weeknight, Make again.
      • Add your own notes, and see how often you've cooked something.

      Where recipes come from

      The books you have, plus the sites you use.

      Your ebook cookbooks

      Point Miso at a folder of EPUB or MOBI files (Google Drive works). It learns each book's layout and pulls out every recipe, photo and cover.

      Tricky books too

      Picture-heavy and scanned-looking cookbooks are read page by page with your Mac's built-in text recognition.

      Favorite recipe sites

      Search NYT Cooking, EatingWell, Love & Lemons, Skinnytaste and Yummy Toddler Food from inside Miso, filter their results, see their ratings, and save a recipe so it's searchable offline.

      Any recipe link

      Paste a link from most recipe sites, or use the Save to Miso bookmark on sites that don't let apps read their pages.

      Get it

      It runs on your own Mac.

      Miso isn't a website you sign up for. It's a small app you run at home, so it can read your own cookbook files.

      • A MacRecent macOS with Python 3 (it comes with Apple's developer tools).
      • Your cookbooksEPUB or MOBI files in one folder. Books with copy protection (DRM) can't be read.
      • Google ChromeOnly needed for picture-style books that have to be read page by page.
      • CalibreOptional, for MOBI and Kindle files.
      1. Ask for access. The code lives in a private GitHub repository; ask for an invite to miso.
      2. Download it and edit config.json to point at your cookbook folder.
      3. Double-click Miso.command. Your browser opens to Miso while it reads your books in the background.
      4. Cook from the iPad by opening the address shown in Settings on the same Wi-Fi, then Add to Home Screen.

      Your books and recipes stay on your computer. Miso only goes online when you search or save from a recipe site or a link you paste.
